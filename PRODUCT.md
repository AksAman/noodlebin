# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Blender artists who build shader, geometry-node, and compositor graphs. They arrive in two moods: browsing for a graph worth stealing (a gallery visit, often on a laptop between renders), or mid-task with a selection on their clipboard that they want to show someone or move to another file. Both end the same way: copy in the browser, paste into Blender.

## Product Purpose

NodeVault turns a Blender node selection into something you can paste into a web page, look at, share as a link, and paste back into Blender with values, links, frames, and groups intact. Success is a stranger opening a post, pressing Copy, and having the exact graph in their own file seconds later. The gallery exists to make that moment happen more often.

## Brand Personality

Curated, exacting, tactile. The work is the hero, the way an image-first collection site (Cosmos) lets the pictures speak; the chrome around it has the quiet precision of Linear or Vercel. Confident without being loud. Speaks to artists as peers who know Blender, never explains what a node is.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages: centered hero, gradient blobs, three feature cards.
- Neon or cyberpunk "creative coding" styling.
- Bland default gray dev tooling with no point of view.
- Cluttered asset marketplaces (Gumroad, Blender Market): prices, badges, ratings, busy tiles.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the graph.** Every surface leads with the actual work: a cover, a render, or the graph itself. Never a placeholder where the work could be.
2. **Copy is the verb.** The path from seeing a graph to having it in Blender is one obvious action on every page that shows one.
3. **Quiet chrome, loud content.** Interface elements recede so covers and node colors carry the color.
4. **Speak Blender.** Use Blender's own vocabulary (tree types, versions, groups, frames) precisely and without explanation.
5. **The canvas is Blender's.** The graph viewport keeps Blender's dark node-editor look regardless of site theme, so what you see is what you paste.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard navigation with visible focus. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and `prefers-color-scheme`; users can override the theme (light, dark, system). Never rely on node or tree-type color alone to carry meaning; pair it with a text label.
